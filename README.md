The port auction sold Pearl Street. Dani Cruz, fixer Ines Vega, signal expert Luz Moreno, driver Tomas Ruiz, and pilot Mara Sol have twelve jobs to steal back the evidence and the reserve. An original offline 3D crime game with a coastal city that changes through four seasons.

Run `node serve.ts` with Node 22.18+ and open `http://localhost:8080`. No installation, build step, account, or network access is needed to play. All geometry, textures, music, and effects are generated by the game. Three.js is vendored locally under its MIT license.

## Play

Enter starts; J opens a contact's briefing and J again accepts it. Follow the teal marker and minimap. Read each job: arriving is only the beginning. Jobs have timers and fail states; J retries a failed job. Completed jobs and purchases persist locally.

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W / S, A / D | Move or drive; forward / reverse and turn |
| Mouse | Aim on foot after clicking the world |
| E | Enter / exit a nearby car or helicopter; use a shop or safehouse |
| Shift | Sprint / vehicle NOS |
| Space | Handbrake; helicopter lift |
| H / Ctrl | Helicopter ascend / descend |
| Click / K | Fire the equipped weapon on foot |
| 1 / 2 / 3, V | Equip owned pistol / shotgun / rifle; cycle |
| Q | Brace behind a nearby car for reduced damage |
| P | Photograph a clear view during the tail job |
| Hold F | Copy a cipher or open the heist vault at its marker |
| C | Start a street race at an amber grid; abandon an active race |
| B | Pay a $450 bribe while out of police sight |
| T / R / M | Cycle seasons / toggle original radio music / map scale |
| Escape | Close an overlay or pause |

## Systems

- Twelve linked story jobs: tail and photograph, timed three-stop delivery and evasion, armed exchange, moving courier interception, relay defense, sightline escape, helicopter rooftop extraction, hold-to-hack cipher, reserve van robbery and physical case collection, two-wave broadcast defense, city checkpoint getaway, and a two-phase port vault heist.
- Sight-based police use building occlusion, view cones, fog-adjusted range, last-seen positions, street routes, searching, cooldown, roadblocks and helicopter support. Bribes, unseen repainting and the Pearl House safehouse give different escape options.
- Kestrel 9 pistol, seven-pellet Breaker 12 shotgun, and precision Longline .30 rifle have different ranges, damage, spread, cadence, ammunition and sounds. Shops sell all three alongside engine, armor and NOS upgrades.
- The city has dense downtown blocks, rooftop AC units and antennas, two usable helipads, an airport with runway and aircraft, a yacht marina, traffic, pedestrians, road signals and waterfront palms.
- Spring blossoms and showers, summer clear skies and storms, autumn leaves and fog, winter snow. Wet roads reduce traction; fog reduces police sight; thunderstorms flash and rumble. A continuous clock changes sunlight, windows, headlights and neon.
- Three checkpoint street races with countdowns, cash rewards and saved best times; fifteen physical hidden ledgers; three ramp jumps with slow motion and first-landing cash rewards. HUD counters show all three activities.
- SOL WAVE 98.4 has original synthesized music and reactive text reports by Niko. Local saves remember story progress, cash, weapons, ammunition, upgrades, packages, races and jumps.

## Captures and handoff

`shots.json` records the four seasonal scenes. They are real browser renders of the same game and HUD; their initial state and camera are staged. `node tools/capture.mjs` connects to a locally running Chromium DevTools endpoint. It captures 960×600 JPEGs directly without image editing.

`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts` builds the deterministic handoff: `game/`, `screenshots/`, and a canonical `dossier.json` using the unchanged kit helper. The kit's server performs the actual judge comparison. No rank or win rate is claimed by this repository.

`node tools/playtest.mjs` runs browser checks for blocked sightlines, objective gates, mission retries and success paths, weapon damage, physical activities and shop persistence. Set `CDP_URL` to your Chromium debug endpoint and `GAME_URL` to a local test port. The tools can start their own local server; they do not need external services.

Built as R3 in one recorded agent session. Do not seal or submit as part of the build; the parent run handles that handoff.
